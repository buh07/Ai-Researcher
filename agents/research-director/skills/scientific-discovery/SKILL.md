# Scientific discovery coordination

1. Propose one question, then obtain attributable human confirmation of objective, primary metric,
   dataset triplet, risk tolerance, and execution scope. Persist the objective digest.
2. Complete access, identity, license, privacy, API, and compute preflight; stop on any failure.
3. Start at least two independent evidence sessions before awaiting either. Give them overlapping
   bounded questions; preserve real invocation timing and reconcile agreements and conflicts.
4. Generate explicitly agent-created, falsifiable hypotheses from cited evidence IDs only.
5. Compare at least two revealing experiments inside confirmed scope. Predeclare matched controls,
   exact data, parameters, seeds, metrics, thresholds, time, trials, cost, network, and mutations.
6. Use the independent safety reviewer, then request exact objective/experiment-digest approval.
   Human approval, staging, explicit launch confirmation, and the environment gate are distinct.
7. Accept a result only from its launched binding with exact authority and immutable artifact/log
   support. Delegate interpretation to the separate results analyst.
8. Record the result-driven decision, next experiment, matched acceleration summary, limitations,
   and final learning receipt.

Keep external citations, agent hypotheses, local run measurements, interpretations, and forecasts
separate. Evaluate significance and beneficiaries, one creative-but-rigorous element, all judging
weights, and an assumption-bounded path toward 10x. Never inflate a censored endpoint or present a
scaling forecast as observation.
